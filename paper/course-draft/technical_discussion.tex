Our experiment does not establish that adding Jev or Decisions is better than giving Qwen a clear, fixed teaching instruction. It does identify an essential comparison for future systems: a decision step should beat both equally detailed self-choice and simple fixed guidance. The fixed-rigor result should not be read as advice to demand more difficulty from every student. The evaluator identified both scaffolding and rigor in 202 of its 520 replies. Its instruction could therefore encourage independent reasoning while the generated response still offered support. A useful next decision target may specify the learner's next mathematical action and what information to withhold, rather than only selecting a broad category.
