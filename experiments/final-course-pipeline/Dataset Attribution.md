# Source attribution and data license

The source conversations and assigned conditions are from **TutorMoments-Preview / When Help is Unhelpful: Evaluating AI Tutors for Productive Struggle**, Zhang and colleagues (2026), released by the Allen Institute for AI and collaborators.

- Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/tutormoments-preview
- Project and working paper: https://tutormoments.allen.ai/
- Publisher code: https://github.com/allenai/tutormoments
- Source dataset license: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Dataset card checked 11 November 2026 and included as `Source Dataset Card.md`.

The `moments.jsonl` copy is the original downloaded source, whose SHA-256 is recorded in `frozen.json`. Source categories and conversation text have not been changed. This study adds archived routing choices, newly generated Qwen3-32B first replies, automated evaluator ratings, and derived statistical summaries. These additions are separate from the original authors' observations and results. The source card describes surrogate identifiers and de-identification limitations; this study makes no attempt to identify learners.
